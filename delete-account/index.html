Delete Your Account

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Todochi account, developed by Lagooners, and what happens to your data when you do.

Delete your account from inside the app

This is the fastest way, and it takes effect immediately.

  1. Open Todochi and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Tap the person icon in the bottom bar.
  3. Tap Manage.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm when prompted. If you signed up with an email address and password, you will be asked for your password. If you signed up with Google or Apple, you will be asked to sign in again to confirm it is you.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, or cannot sign in, you can ask us to delete your account for you.

Email lagoonersofficial@gmail.com with the subject line “Delete my Todochi account”, and include the email address you used to sign up.

We will confirm your request and delete the account within 30 days. We may need to contact you to verify that the account is yours before we proceed.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account (email address, name, user ID) Permanently deleted from our authentication provider
Your profile (display name, chosen character, onboarding status) Permanently deleted from our database
Your reminders, subtasks and completion history Deleted from the device the app is installed on
Scheduled alarms Cancelled

Deletion is permanent. We cannot restore an account or its data once it has been deleted, and signing up again creates a new, empty account.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Custom voices you recorded, and any photos you added to them. These are stored only on your own device and are never uploaded to us. Deleting your account does not remove them. To remove them, delete them in the app or uninstall Todochi.
  • Purchase and subscription records. If you subscribed to Todochi Premium, your purchase is recorded by the app store you bought it from and by our subscription provider, RevenueCat. We do not control these records and they are retained according to those providers' own policies, and to the extent required by tax and accounting law. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription — see below.
  • Support correspondence. If you have emailed us, that email thread is retained for up to 24 months.

Cancel your subscription separately

Deleting your Todochi account does not cancel an active subscription, and uninstalling the app does not either. Cancel it through the subscription settings of the app store you bought it from, before deleting your account.

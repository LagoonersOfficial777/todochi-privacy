Todochi

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Todochi ("the App"), developed by Lagooners, collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information.

Information We Collect

Account information

You can use Todochi as a guest, or create an account using email and password, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple. If you create an account, we collect and store:

  • Your email address
  • The name, age, and country you provide during profile setup
  • Your selected companion character and app preferences

If you sign in as a guest without creating an account, this information is instead stored locally on your device only, and is not sent to our servers.

Reminder and usage content

Reminders, subtasks, and completion history you create in the App are stored locally on your device using an on-device database. This content is not currently synced to our servers.

Purchase information

If you subscribe to Todochi Premium, purchase and subscription status is processed by the in-app purchase system of the app store from which you obtained the App, and managed with the help of RevenueCat, our subscription management provider. RevenueCat receives a unique identifier tied to your account so we can verify your subscription status; it does not receive your payment card details, which are handled entirely by your app store provider.

Device permissions

Todochi requests permissions for notifications, exact alarms, and full-screen alerts so that reminders can wake your device reliably. These permissions are used only to deliver reminders and are not used to collect or share data about you.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To sync your profile and character selection across devices, if you have an account
  • To verify and manage your premium subscription
  • To deliver reminders and alarms as you've scheduled them
  • To respond to support requests you send us

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell your information to third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the App's Settings screen. Deleting your account removes your profile data from our servers. Note that subscription cancellation is handled separately through Apple's or Google's own subscription management, accessible from the App's Settings.

Children's Privacy

Todochi is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated Effective Date above.
